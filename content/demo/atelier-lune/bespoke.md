# Bespoke & Gifting

For weddings, corporate gifts, and private events, we offer bespoke candle orders starting at **20 units**.

## What's included

Choose any scent from our collection, poured into our standard smoked-glass jar. Each candle can be finished with a **wax seal** in your choice of color, stamped with a custom monogram or motif.

## What's not available

Glass engraving is not offered for bespoke orders — only the wax seal can be personalized.

## Timeline

Bespoke orders take **3 weeks** from confirmation to shipping, so please plan ahead for weddings and seasonal events. We recommend placing your order at least 6 weeks before the date you need it, to leave room for shipping.

## Getting started

Reach out through our contact page with your quantity, scent choice, and seal design, and we'll send a quote.
