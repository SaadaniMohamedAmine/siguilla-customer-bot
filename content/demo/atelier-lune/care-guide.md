# Candle Care Guide

Our candles are poured from a blend of rapeseed and beeswax — a few habits will get you the cleanest burn and the longest life out of every jar.

## First burn

Let the first burn last **2 to 3 hours**, long enough for the wax to melt evenly all the way to the edge of the jar. A short first burn causes "tunneling" — the candle will only ever melt down the middle after that.

## Wick care

Trim the wick to **5 mm** before every burn. A long or curled wick causes soot and an uneven flame.

## Burn time

Never burn a candle for more than **4 hours at a stretch**. Let it cool completely, re-trim the wick, and relight.

## Storage

Keep candles away from direct sunlight and drafts, and store them upright below 25°C.
