# FAQ

**Are your candles vegan?**
Yes — our blend of rapeseed and beeswax contains no animal products besides the beeswax itself; if you need a fully vegan option, ask us about our soy-only blend.

**How long does a full-size candle burn?**
Between 50 and 60 hours depending on the scent — see Our Collections for exact times.

**Can I reuse the jar?**
Yes, our smoked-glass jars are designed to be washed and reused once the wax is gone.

**Do you ship internationally?**
Yes — see our Shipping Policy for exact delivery times by region.

**Can I return a lit candle?**
No — only unlit, unopened candles in original packaging are eligible for return.

**How do I clean wax drips off the jar?**
Warm water and a cloth — the wax softens easily and wipes off once the jar has cooled.

**Are your fragrances phthalate-free?**
Yes, all our fragrance oils are phthalate-free.

**Do you restock sold-out scents?**
Our four core scents are always in stock; seasonal editions do not return once sold out.

**Can I track my order?**
Yes, a tracking link is emailed once your order ships.

**Do you offer wholesale pricing?**
Not currently, but bespoke orders of 20+ units get a volume discount — see Bespoke & Gifting.
