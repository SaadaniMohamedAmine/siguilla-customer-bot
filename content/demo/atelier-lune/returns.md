# Returns & Exchanges

We want you to love what you burn. If something isn't right, you have 30 days from delivery to return it.

## Conditions

- Candles must be **unlit and unopened** — once a wick has been trimmed or lit, we can't resell it, and for hygiene reasons we can't accept it back.
- Engraved or personalized glass jars (see Bespoke & Gifting) are final sale and cannot be returned.
- Items must be in their original packaging.

## How refunds work

Once we receive your return, we inspect it and issue a refund to your original payment method within 7 business days. Return shipping is at your own cost unless the item arrived damaged or incorrect.

## Exchanges

Prefer a different scent or size? Let us know within the 30-day window and we'll swap it, provided the original is unlit and unopened.
