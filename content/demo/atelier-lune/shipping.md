# Shipping Policy

We pack every order by hand in our Paris atelier and ship within 24 hours on business days.

## Delivery times

- **France** — 1–2 business days.
- **European Union** — 2–4 business days.
- **Switzerland** — 3–5 business days. Duties and VAT are calculated and collected at checkout, so there's nothing to pay on delivery.
- **United Kingdom** — 3–5 business days.
- **United States** — 5–8 business days.

## Free shipping

Orders of 120 € or more ship free, anywhere we deliver.

## Tracking

A tracking link is emailed as soon as your order leaves the atelier. Candles are fragile — we pack them in reinforced boxes with wood-wool cushioning, but if anything arrives damaged, contact us within 48 hours and we'll send a replacement at no charge.
