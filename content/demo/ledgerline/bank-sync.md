# Bank sync

## Connecting an account

From Settings → Banking, search for your bank and log in through the secure connection flow. Ledgerline never stores your banking password — it uses a read-only, revocable connection.

## How matching works

Each incoming transaction is automatically matched against open invoices by amount and payer name. A matched transaction marks the invoice as Paid; unmatched ones land in a review queue for you to categorize.

## Categorizing expenses

Uncategorized transactions can be assigned to an expense category manually, or you can set a rule (e.g. "always categorize payments to AWS as Hosting") so future transactions match automatically.

## Refresh frequency

Connected accounts sync automatically once a day; you can also trigger a manual refresh anytime from the Banking tab.
