# Getting started with Ledgerline

Ledgerline helps small businesses send invoices, reconcile bank transactions, and stay VAT-compliant without hiring an accountant for every little thing.

## Creating your workspace

Sign up with your business email, pick your country (this sets your default VAT rules), and connect your first bank account.

## Your first invoice

From the Invoices tab, click **New invoice**, add a client and line items, and send it by email or share a payment link. Ledgerline tracks whether it's been viewed and paid.

## Inviting your team

Add teammates from Settings → Members. Each person gets a role: Admin (full access), Bookkeeper (invoices and bank sync, no billing), or Viewer (read-only reports).
