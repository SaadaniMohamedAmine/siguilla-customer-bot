# Invoices

## Creating an invoice

Add a client (or pick an existing one), line items with quantity and unit price, and an optional due date. Ledgerline calculates VAT automatically based on your client's country and your tax settings.

## Sending and tracking

Invoices can be emailed directly or shared as a link. Each invoice shows a status: Draft, Sent, Viewed, Paid, or Overdue.

## Recurring invoices

For retainer clients, mark an invoice as recurring (weekly, monthly, or yearly) and Ledgerline generates and sends it automatically on schedule.

## Reminders

Overdue invoices trigger an automatic reminder email after 7 and 14 days, which you can customize or turn off per client.

## Credit notes

To correct or cancel a sent invoice, issue a credit note referencing the original rather than deleting it — this keeps your VAT records clean.
