# Plans & billing

## Plans

Ledgerline has three plans: **Starter** (1 user, 20 invoices/month), **Growth** (5 users, unlimited invoices, bank sync), and **Scale** (unlimited users, multi-currency, priority support).

## Changing plans

Upgrade or downgrade anytime from Settings → Billing. Changes are prorated — you're only charged the difference for the rest of your billing cycle.

## Invoices for your own subscription

Your Ledgerline subscription invoice is available under Settings → Billing → Invoice history, and a new one is added automatically each billing cycle.

## Cancelling

You can cancel anytime; your workspace stays active (read-only for invoicing) until the end of your current billing period, and your data is kept for 90 days in case you come back.
