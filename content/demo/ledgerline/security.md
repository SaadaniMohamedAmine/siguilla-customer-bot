# Security

## How your data is protected

All data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Bank connections are read-only and can be revoked at any time from Settings → Banking — Ledgerline never has the ability to move money out of your account.

## Two-factor authentication

Enable 2FA from Settings → Security to require a one-time code at sign-in, in addition to your password.

## Team access

Each teammate only sees what their role allows — see Getting Started for the three role levels. Removing a teammate immediately revokes their access.

## Data export

You can export your invoices and transaction history as CSV at any time from Settings → Data export, independent of your subscription status.
