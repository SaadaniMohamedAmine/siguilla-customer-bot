# VAT

## Setting your VAT rate

Set your default VAT rate in Settings → Tax when you create your workspace. Ledgerline supports standard, reduced, and zero rates, and applies the right one based on your client's country and whether they're a business or a consumer.

## Reverse charge

For B2B invoices to clients in other EU countries, Ledgerline automatically applies the reverse-charge mechanism and adds the required mention to the invoice, provided your client's VAT number is on file.

## VAT returns

The Reports tab generates a VAT summary for any period, broken down by rate and by country — ready to hand to your accountant or file yourself, depending on your local requirements.

## Changing your rate

If your VAT rate changes (a new reduced-rate category, for instance), update it in Settings — it only affects invoices created after the change; past invoices keep the rate that applied when they were issued.
