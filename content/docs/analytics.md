# Analytics

Each agent's Overview and the Analytics page report four numbers, over 7, 30, or 90 days:

- **Conversations** — count of visitor conversations started in the period (playground conversations are always excluded).
- **Resolved without a human** — the share of those conversations that never reached a handoff.
- **Satisfaction** — thumbs-up divided by (thumbs-up + thumbs-down) on bot answers.
- **Fallback rate** — bot answers that declined, divided by all bot answers.

You'll also find a conversations-per-day chart, the most-cited sources, your top questions, and unanswered questions worth covering with new content.
