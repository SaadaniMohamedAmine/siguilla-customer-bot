# Customizing the widget

The Widget tab gives you a live preview of every change.

- **Color** — pick a preset or any hex value. Text on top of your color automatically switches between light and dark ink for contrast.
- **Theme** — dark or light.
- **Position** — bottom right or bottom left.
- **Header style** — subtle or bold.
- **Avatar** — upload an image, or keep the default monogram.
- **Messages** — welcome message, input placeholder, up to 3 suggested questions, and an optional launcher tooltip.
- **Badge** — the "Sealed by Sigilla" badge can be hidden on Pro and Team plans.

Changes only reach visitors once you click **Publish changes**.
