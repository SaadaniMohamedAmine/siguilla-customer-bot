# Human handoff

Sometimes a visitor needs a person, not an agent.

## Triggering a handoff

- The visitor can ask for a human directly from the widget.
- Sigilla triggers it automatically after 2 consecutive fallback answers in the same conversation.

In both cases, the visitor can leave an email address so your team can follow up even if they close the tab.

## Taking over

Pending handoffs show up in **Conversations**, with a badge in the sidebar. Click **Take over** to join the conversation — your agent stops auto-replying, and your messages appear in the widget within a few seconds.

## Resolving

Click **Resolve** once the conversation is handled. If the visitor sends a new message afterward, your agent picks it back up automatically.
