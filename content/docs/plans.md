## Monthly quota

Messages reset on the 1st of each month (UTC). Once you reach your plan's limit, agents rest until the next period or until you upgrade.

## Downgrading

Nothing is ever deleted when you downgrade. Agents, sources and members beyond your new plan's limit become read-only until you upgrade again or remove some.
